Use the five cultural images as the core set. The airport image is optional and should appear only on Day 10 at secondary scale. ## Recommended visual pairing

- **Day 06:** wide Kiyomizu panorama as the header; Fushimi torii and Ninenzaka below it in a restrained two-card row.
- **Day 07:** Arashiyama as the dominant image; no repeated Higashiyama image unless the day sheet needs a small evening-Kyoto accent.
- **Day 08:** Osaka Castle in daylight paired with Dotonbori at night. The contrast communicates the day's structure immediately.
- **Day 09:** reuse Dotonbori only if the sheet lacks a Kuromon/Umeda-specific image; avoid repeating both Day 08 images at full size.
- **Day 10:** airport image is optional. A small landscape crop beside practical departure details is enough.
