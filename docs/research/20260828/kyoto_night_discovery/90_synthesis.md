# Kyoto night photograph shortlist

## Recommendation

Use **Kyoto Gion At Night** by Harald Johnsen as the primary replacement. It directly fixes the current mismatch: the scene is unquestionably nighttime, visually specific to Kyoto, elegant rather than touristy, and the illuminated Yasaka-no-Tō pagoda gives the day-sheet a clear focal point. The warm amber palette also matches the site’s lacquer/gold visual language.
