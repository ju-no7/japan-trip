# When cities are near +24 C

Derived from `20_sources.md`, official JMA 1991-2020 climate normals; these describe mean daily maxima/minima, not a forecast or a constant temperature.

| City | May max/min C | September max/min C | October max/min C |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tokyo | 23.6 / 14.6 | 27.5 / 20.3 | 22.0 / 14.8 |
| Kyoto | 25.1 / 14.5 | 29.2 / 20.7 | 23.4 / 14.4 |
| Osaka | 24.9 / 16.0 | 29.5 / 21.9 | 23.7 / 16.0 |

Tokyo specifically: October 1 has normal maximum/minimum 24.5/17.4 C, October 4 24.0/17.0 C, October 10 23.0/16.0 C. Thus early October is a closer match to +24 C daytime in Tokyo than the currently tentative October 17-26 window (Tokyo normal maxima 21.7 declining to 20.3 C). May also matches about +24-25 C daytime across all three cities. This is an inference from climate normals, not a recommendation that includes Fuji visibility or crowds, which require separate evidence.
